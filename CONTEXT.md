# Grundle Ball

Grundle Ball is the companion product for the Grundle League. This glossary distinguishes the product, the league, and the playoff formats it presents.

## Language

**Grundle Ball**:
The companion product for Grundle League standings, matchups, rules, and playoff information.
_Avoid_: Keeper Bowl Playoffs

**Grundle League**:
The 12-team Sleeper fantasy football keeper league whose competition and rules Grundle Ball presents.
_Avoid_: Grundle Ball when referring to the league itself

**Team**:
A persistent Grundle League franchise whose roster, draft picks, keeper history, and obligations survive a change of human Manager.
_Avoid_: Manager when referring to the franchise

**Canonical Draft**:
The annual draft referenced by a season's Sleeper league record and treated as that season's official draftboard.
_Avoid_: Abandoned draft, mock draft

**Keeper Designation**:
A player assigned to a Team as a keeper in a particular season's Canonical Draft. A current designation may remain provisional until that draft is finalized.
_Avoid_: Rostered player, keeper-eligible player

**Keeper Season**:
One finalized Keeper Designation for a particular Team, player, and season.
_Avoid_: Keeper year

**Official Playoffs**:
The championship and consolation brackets configured by Sleeper and used to determine the league's final standings.
_Avoid_: Grundle Bowl, Keeper Bowl Playoffs

**Grundle Bowl**:
The proposed three-bracket house-rule playoff format retained as a non-authoritative beta after the league voted it down for 2026.
_Avoid_: Official Playoffs, Keeper Bowl Playoffs

**Keeper Bowl**:
The middle bracket within the Grundle Bowl format, fed by teams from its Champ Bowl and Toilet Bowl brackets. It is not the product name.
_Avoid_: Keeper Bowl Playoffs when referring to Grundle Ball
